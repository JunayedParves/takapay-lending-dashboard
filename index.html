                            Data notes and assumptions

                            • Instalments due after 31 Jul 2026 carry a placeholder 15% partial payment in the source, so collection measures stop at July 2026 and delinquency on those instalments is not yet visible.
                            • The documented benchmark GLP (৳1,239.9M) includes ৳60.5M on written-off loans; this dashboard excludes written-off principal, as the definition in the metric guide states.
                            • The documented PAR uses each loan’s worst-ever DPD; this dashboard uses DPD at month-end so history can be trended.
                            • Geography is the borrower’s registered location. Penetration uses registered customers as the denominator.
                            • Division boundaries come from geoBoundaries (gbOpen, BGD ADM1, simplified); they are for display, not survey accuracy.
                            • All customer, loan and agent records are synthetic; loan IDs are shown without personal data.

                            Predictive model methodology

                            • Both models are L2-regularised logistic regressions fitted by Newton’s method in Python (numpy), on standardised numeric features and one-hot categories.
                            • PD features at origination: loan amount, tenure, rate, product, purpose, loan cycle, credit score, age, income, debt-to-income, loan-to-income, existing balance, account tenure, earlier late and missed instalments and worst DPD (only instalments due before disbursement), historical on-time ratio, wallet balance, transaction count and savings activity from the latest monthly snapshot, occupation, division, urban/rural, education, gender, KYC and channel.
                            • Early-warning features at each month-end: months on book, share repaid, late and partial instalments on the loan so far, earlier repayment history, current wallet activity and the origination features above.
                            • Validation is out of time: every test record is later than every training record. Risk thresholds: PD segments at 2%, 5% and 10%; alert threshold at the training population’s 85th percentile.
                            • SHAP values for a linear model are coefficient × (value − mean); importance is their mean absolute size. They describe the model, not cause and effect.
